What it's about

What it will do

  • The mock-up opens full screen. "Open the mock-up" opens it as its own page, at the screen's real width, with its scripts on. On a phone that link replaces the box; on a wide screen the box stays, as tall as the screen allows, with the same link.
  • The dock is one row on a phone: the count and the button side by side, about 60 px.
  • The top bar steps aside while reading: on a phone it slides away when the owner scrolls down and comes back as soon as they scroll up.
  • Together: about 560 px of a phone's screen for the points instead of about 350.
