## Outcome

A paired display at `/display/` shows its space full screen:
- the current photo and/or note, with the space's 3–5 reaction emoji underneath
- nothing else to tap, and the screen stays awake (Wake Lock where available)

**Live updates:**
- A new moment fades in within a second or two of being posted.
- A tap sends a reaction, and every display in the space briefly shows it floating up.

**Offline:**
- With the server off, the display keeps showing the last moment.
- Taps are queued on the device and delivered when the server is back.

**Revocation:** revoking the display in admin cuts its live connection within 2 s (ADR 00 §2.2).

**History, names and language ([ADR 09](../adr/09-display-history-and-names.md), added 2026-09-29):**
- ‹ › arrows (and swiping) through the space's whole history; back to the latest after 2 minutes idle, and a "New ›" hint when a moment arrives while someone is browsing.
- Who posted and when under each moment, the names of who reacted in a quiet line, and names on the floating emoji.
- A small ⚙ in a corner to switch the display between English and Français.

**Posting:** there is no sender app yet (M4). Moments are posted with a dev/staging command, with or without a photo, through the same service M4's upload will use.

## Design choices in this plan

These don't depart from an ADR. Tell me if you want any of them changed.

1. **Reactions are sent over HTTP, and SSE pushes to the display** (ADR 06).
   - `POST /api/events` is idempotent by the event's UUID (`coucou/services.py`), so the outbox can simply retry.
   - M4's sender uses the same endpoint.
2. **The DB watcher also pushes new events.**
   - Every second, it polls for events received since its last look, as well as `AuthGeneration`.
   - This way, events written by another process (the dev command now, admin later) reach displays too.
3. **The photo pipeline lands in M3, not M4.**
   - The pipeline: re-encode without metadata, keep the original, make a 3072 px display copy, and serve that copy to authenticated devices only (ADR 00 §2.6).
   - The display can't be tested without photos.
   - M4 then only adds the upload itself and the phone's outbox, which is already the hardest part for iOS.
