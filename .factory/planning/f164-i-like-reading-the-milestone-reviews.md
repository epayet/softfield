## The lead, 2026-10-21 09:02

> I've drafted the plan for F164 on its branch: a mock-up and three tickets so you can plant a review's ideas from the review itself.
>
> 1. **Buttons only on the ideas the review writes as a ready note**: A review's suggestions come two ways. Some are a quoted line written to be planted: F19's 'Plans: every Done-when line…', which you copied by hand and is now F166, F28's 'Gate: each new test…' and F135's 'Needs you: each review stop says…'. Others are the lead talking in passing, like F19's 'Checkpoints on template work should list…'. A button on every bullet would plant half-sentences that make poor cards. Instead, every review will write each suggestion that needs a card as one quoted sentence, in the voice of your Plant box notes, and only those get buttons. Suggestions the review already handled itself (a new rule in the lead's notes) stay as plain words. You can still copy anything else into the Plant box.
>    Action: Ticket f164-01 (lead): the review's brief and the lead's sign-off sheet rule ask for it
> 2. **Where the buttons show**: In two places. On the card's thread, where you read the review and sign off, a new 'Ideas from this review · N' list sits after the sections. On the whole review page, each idea line becomes a plate right where it stood, instead of today's '> …' text. Each plate has Plant and Plant and ask the lead, the same two buttons as the Plant box. When you press one, the plate turns dashed yellow, 'On its way', until the runner's next pick. Then it links to the new card, whose notes end 'From F28's review.' The mock-up shows F19's review: Today, then Proposed with one idea already planted and one you can press.
>    Action: Mock-up review-ideas.html; ticket f164-03 builds it
> 3. **Ideas you already planted by hand show as planted**: An idea counts as planted when a plant has its words, with or without the final full stop. So F19's Plans idea will show 'Planted · F166' with a link at once, and you won't plant it twice. Older reviews (F19, F28, F135) get their buttons as soon as this lands; no re-review is needed.
>    Action: In f164-02
> 4. **Factory ideas from another project's review land on Softfield's board**: Travel companion's M1 review suggested a browser check for developers, and you planted it on Softfield as F163. So a Factory idea from any project's review plants on Softfield's board, and its plate says 'for Softfield'. A Product idea plants on the review's own project. Tell me if you'd rather keep every idea on its own project.
>    Action: Decision D2 in the plan
> 5. **Plant and ask the lead costs a planning job each time**: Each 'ask' press starts an Opus planning job on the new card, the most expensive kind of job (about $107 of Claude's spend since 8 Oct). Plain Plant costs nothing and waits on the board until you write on its thread. My suggestion: use Plant for ideas you'd like kept, and ask only when you want the lead's answer now. Both buttons are there either way.
>    Action: Nothing
> 6. **Plan: three tickets, two of them mine**: 01 (lead, small): reviews write their ideas as quoted lines. It's mine because the review's brief is protected. 02 (developer): read the ideas from the stored review and work out whether each is planted, on its way or refused. That is plain Python with tests, and the code that finds a sign-off's state is the example to follow. 03 (lead, after 02): the plates and buttons on both pages. F19's and F28's reviews found that page-and-script tickets on these pages cost developers about two hours each for a few dozen lines, and asked for the lead to take them. Nothing in the queue changes, so there's no need to pause.
