## Decisions
- **D1: the mark lives in the card's header,** `**Fast lane:** yes` after its Area, and nowhere else. `milestone.card` reads it into `Card.reserve` (False when missing). Only the runner writes it.
- **D2: the request is `reserve` with a card's short key as subject** (`f142`, value `on` / `off`), beside `week` and `five_hour`. `runner/requests.py` tells them apart; `factory_dashboard/requests.py` doesn't change.
- **D3: who passes:** `loop.reserve_cards(repo, ready) -> set[str]`: the ids of the ready lead tickets of marked cards, plus every ready ticket they wait for through `depends_on`, transitively and across cards. Neither `at_reserve` nor `at_five_hour` parks a ticket in that set, and `_waits_for_the_owner` (the order in `_next_task`) treats it as free (owner, 2026-10-22: both reserves). A real limit (a window at 100%, a refusal resting it) still holds it.
- **D4: marking frees what is parked:** the request applied, the card's tickets parked with kind `reserve` or `five-hour` (and those they wait for) get `RETRY`, and their `reserve` and `five-hour` owner items are resolved, as `_week_switched_on` and `_five_hour_switched_on` do for all.
- **D5: the mark ends at sign-off:** the sign-off's rewrite of the card doc drops the `**Fast lane:**` field. Until then a card with no ready ticket lets nothing run.
- **D6: what the page shows:** the card's page has a lever like the allowance panel's (`.lever`, `factory_dashboard/templates/_partials/allowance.html`), only while the card has ready tickets; the board's planted and growing cards show a small "uses reserve" plate; the Overview names the marked cards once, under Claude's two meters. A lever pulled shows on its way (dashed orange edge) until the runner applies it.
- **D7: the fast lane goes first** (ADR 35 items 2, 3 and 5): `dispatch.plan` and `dispatch.pick` take `first: Collection[str]`, the ids of `reserve_cards`. A ticket in it, and a queued direction or escalation whose ticket is in it, rank before every queued kind and every other ticket; the rest keep today's order. `_next_task` picks from the set first, before a parked ticket whose reset has passed, in `pick_next`'s order (the board's, with F173's pulls). The owner's pause holds them as it holds every ticket; a real limit too, and the line takes the next job meanwhile.
- **D8: the lever lives on `/milestones/<key>`** (ADR 35 item 6): a "Fast lane" row in `#card-head`'s `dl`, after Tickets, only on a planted card with tickets left (`board.RESERVABLE`); `thread.html` loses its `#reserve-bay`. `_reserve_lever` moves from `factory_dashboard/planning.py` to where the milestone page is built (`factory_dashboard/milestones.py`). The board's plate reads "fast lane"; the Overview's line under Claude's meters reads "Fast lane:" and links to `/milestones/<full key>`.
- **D9: Next in line shows the lane on top** (ADR 35 item 6), above the queued jobs, in a striped bay headed "Fast lane". The order comes from `board.reserve_marked` and the run order, as the runner's (D7); no new table.
- **D10: the name in the card** (ADR 35 item 1): the header line is `**Fast lane:** yes`, which D1's `Card.reserve` reads (the attribute keeps its name; only words change). Every card doc with the old mark is rewritten in the same change, and `card` still reads the old form as the lane, so a mark the runner writes while the ticket runs isn't lost; the request stays `reserve` with a card's key (D2), and the runner's commit reads "F142 in the fast lane, by the owner" / "F142 out of the fast lane, by the owner". Owner items, logs and the Needs-you text say "fast lane" where they named the card's mark.
