## Outcome
- `/` is the factory line, live (refreshes every 5 s): the allowances at the top, Needs you (only when needed), the floor, Now (the running ticket's goal and the next three), and what clogged the line today.
- **The floor, left to right:**
  - the field (seeds, planting, planted rows);
  - the queue hopper with crates in run order;
  - the lead (Opus), the reviewers (Sonnet, Copilot), and the developer bay with one machine per developer agent;
  - checks, merged today, ripe, and the harvest stash.
  - Every belt shows today's count; a belt carrying something now rolls.
- **Phone:** a portrait drawing of the same floor, top to bottom, with dial allowances.
- **Replay** (owner, 2026-10-03, to review what happened overnight): the floor replays a stretch of time, "overnight" by default.
- **Not in this version:** the other tabs, a zoomable map.

## Decisions

- **D11 The live line (supersedes D10 where they differ; owner, 2026-10-02):**
  - **Where:** at `/`. Running now and the Queue panels go; their content moves into the floor and the Now panel. `/milestones/<key>/line` is not built in this version.
  - **Stations** (ids fixed): `seeds`, `planting`, `rows`, `queue`, `lead`, `reviewers` (one machine per agent in `[roles] checkpoint` and `checkpoint-stand-in`), `developers` (one machine per agent in `[roles] developer` and `last-attempt`), `checks`, `merged`, `ripe`, `stash`.
  - **The field counts come from the board** (`factory_dashboard/board.py`, f5-15): seeds are its seed cards, planting its sprouting cards, rows its planted and growing cards, ripe its ripe cards, the stash its harvested ones.
  - **Belts** (ids fixed): `seeds→planting`, `planting→rows`, `rows→queue`, `queue→lead`, `queue→developers`, `lead→checks`, `developers→checks`, `checks→developers` (retry), `checks→lead` (direction or escalation), `checks→merged`, `merged→ripe`, `ripe→stash` (the owner's sign-off), `merged→reviewers` (checkpoints).
  - **Each belt has today's count** (since local midnight), from the trips (`line.py`) and the board's changes. It also has `carrying`: true while a job moves a crate along it now.
  - **A machine's state:** `working` (with its crate: ticket, goal, minutes, attempt), `idle`, `waiting` (a limit, with its reset) or `off` (a role it has lost, such as Sonnet as a developer).
  - **`line.live_line(conn, project, now, at=None)`** (`at`: any past moment, f3-06) returns that document (stations, belts, machines, Now, next three in run order, the clog numbers), all in Python. `factory_dashboard/static/factory.js` only draws it, with the same SVG for desktop and a portrait layout under 640 px.
  - **Motion:** from `.softfield/cards/f1-factory-mockup.html`:
    - rollers march on a carrying belt;
    - smoke rises from a working machine's chimney;
    - the checks light flashes green or red when a result lands.
    - All still under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **What clogged the line today** (f3-04), since local midnight: `queries.clog(conn, project, since, now, machines=None)`, under `live_line(...)["clog"]` as `{limit, failed, failures, idle: [{agent, name, minutes, share}], owner, live}`. Minutes are rounded; only what had happened by `now` counts, so the line at a past moment shows that moment's numbers. **The runner's live time** (`live`) is each run of the project from `started` to `ended` (unfinished: its last heartbeat plus 2 min).
  - **`limit`, waiting for Claude's limit:** the union of every Claude refusal's `at`→`resets_at` (a parked job always follows one: its own row only holds the work before the park) and every `wait` usage event, kept to the runner's live time. A `wait` (agent `runner`, f3-04) is what `runner.loop.wait_for_claude` records when it starts waiting for a limit, a hold on Claude's allowance or nothing to run without Claude, from then to when it resumes; a pause for a passing error isn't one. It exists because `runs.waiting_until` is only the current wait, cleared when it ends, and a hold on the allowance writes no refusal. Before f3-04, those waits weren't recorded.
  - **`failed` and `failures`, failed attempts:** the minutes and the number of developer and last-attempt jobs that ended `failed` (not parked, interrupted, refused or a passing error).
  - **`idle`, machines idle while another job ran:** per developer machine on the floor, the minutes of the runner's live time when some job of the project ran but none of its own; `share` is that over `live` (0 with no live time).
  - **`owner`, stopped for the owner:** the minutes an owner item of the project was open (`created`→`resolved`, or still open) while no job of the project ran, whether a runner lived or not.

- **D5 Charts:** a `<div data-chart="<kind>">` followed by a `<script type="application/json">` data block; `factory_dashboard/static/charts.js` has one function per kind, drawing with ECharts. Tests check the JSON a route renders, not the drawing; the review takes screenshots.
- **D10 The factory line** (the owner, 2026-10-02; the picture is [`f1-factory-mockup.html`](f1-factory-mockup.html), v18, not a file to read):
  - **Where:** the Overview (`/`) becomes the factory, live: Needs-you (only when needed), the floor of the current milestone, the Now panel, then allowances, savings and spend. Running now and Queue as separate panels go. Each milestone has `/milestones/<key>/line`, which replays it in four tabs: Factory floor, Dependencies, Production timeline (replacing `/timeline`) and Plan vs actual. The top bar gets "Factory".
  - **Data, all in Python:** `factory_dashboard/line.py` owns `factory_line(conn, project, milestone)`, a JSON document (D5 style) that the drawing only renders: no rule lives in JavaScript. Tested in `tests/factory_dashboard/test_line.py`. `queries.run_order(conn, project)` (in `factory_dashboard/queries.py`) is `pick_next`'s rule (`runner/tasks.py`) applied repeatedly to the DB's `ready` rows: the order the runner will take them, with "ready now" or "after f1-xx".
  - **Machines:** one per agent in the project's `.softfield/project.toml` that a role names or a job used (`runner.config.load_config` on `projects.repo`), with its name, LOCAL (harness on the GPU) or CLOUD, its `rating` (the latest `rating_changes.after` once f1-24 writes them), and its side: **the lead is one machine**, "The lead (Claude)", with one plate per Claude model it runs on (Opus, Sonnet) and the active one lit; developers share one developer bay; a shadow agent (Copilot, f1-26) has its own blue machine fed by the same checkpoint and "not rated yet" without a rating. Stronger agents are bigger and have a bigger chimney.
  - **Stations and belts** (ids fixed): stations `queue`, the machines, `checks` (the runner's gates, not an agent), `merged`. Belts: `queue→dev` "developer tickets", `queue→lead` "lead tickets", `dev→checks` "attempt done: to checks", `lead→checks` "lead's work: to checks", `checks→merged` "passed: merged", `checks→dev` "failed: retry", `checks→lead` "failed twice: direction · 3 times: escalation", `lead→dev` "a direction: retry", `lead→queue` "back to the queue (findings, requeued, parked)", `merged→lead` "checkpoints, milestone review", `merged→shadow` "same checkpoint".
  - **Trips, from the jobs:** a ticket's first job takes it from the queue to its machine; a job that passes and is the ticket's last goes `→checks→merged`; a failed developer attempt goes `→checks` then `checks→dev` (a retry) or `checks→lead` (a direction or an escalation is next); a direction goes `lead→dev`; a lead ticket parked, interrupted or requeued, and an escalation that requeues, go `lead→queue` and come back on `queue→lead`/`queue→dev`; a checkpoint or review job (no ticket) is a batch crate `merged→lead→queue`. Directions and findings are text, so they skip checks. The JSON lists every trip as `{at, crate, belts, to}`, time-ordered, plus each belt's last and next trip.
  - **What the page shows:** crates coloured by kind (lead orange, developer green), faded and dashed while waiting on a dependency, numbered in run order in the Queue; Merged in completion order; the working ticket's path lit (the belt it came on solid, its way to Merged dashed); a belt rolls only while a crate travels on it; limit windows grey the lead with "Claude limit reached · resets HH:MM"; the Checks gate flashes green or red. Play steps one event at a time (5, 3 or 1.5 s), ◀ ▶ step by hand, no zoom. Hover or tap a crate: its details and its dependencies outlined; a belt: its last and next trip. Under 640 px a portrait drawing fits the width (developers stacked), with "the same, in words" below and a sticky Play bar.
  - **Idle stretches** over 40 min are drawn shorter on the timeline and plan tabs, with their real length written on them.
  - **The drawing:** `factory_dashboard/static/factory.js`, hand-written SVG, no new vendor file; smoke and animation honour `prefers-reduced-motion` (still puffs). The review checks it with screenshots on desktop and phone (D5).
