> 1. **Your reply turns the thread's proposals dashed orange**: Today a message on a thread doesn't change its proposals. They stay solid in "Proposals to review" and are counted in the Planning tab and Needs you, because only your own Approve or Reject dashes a proposal. From the moment you press Send, every open proposal of that thread will turn dashed orange and say "being revised · the lead is writing its reply now" (or when the reply will come). They will stop counting as waiting for you. This shows on the desk, in "Proposals from this card" on the thread page, and on the proposal's own page. They go back to solid when the lead's reply is stored. If that reply brings new proposals, those replace them.

The alternatives are (a) hide the buttons until the lead replies, or (b) keep Reject only.
