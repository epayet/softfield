# F232: A plant waits 2 minutes before it becomes a card, so it can still be edited

The planning thread behind F232's plan, oldest first, as it was when the owner approved it.

## The owner, 2026-11-06 19:25

> I can't edit a seed I just planted that has not been picked up yet anymore

## The lead, 2026-11-09 16:13

> Your edit window went away because plants now become cards about a second after you send them whenever the factory is idle or paused. My plan holds each plant for 2 minutes so you can edit it again.
>
> 1. **Why you couldn't edit it**: F14, Edit or cancel a waiting plant, only works while the runner hasn't taken the plant yet. That used to mean until its next pick, often half an hour away. Since the runner started holding when idle (F101) and when you pause it, it applies your requests every 5 seconds. On 2026-11-06 the factory was idle, and F231, F232, F233 and F234 became cards 0.6 to 5 seconds after you planted them. The Coming seeds row with Save and Cancel it was gone before you could open it. While a ticket runs, a plant still waits for the next pick and can still be edited.
>    Action: Plan f232: hold plants for 2 minutes (points 2–4).
> 2. **Hold the plant for 2 minutes, rather than edit the card afterwards**: With the hold, the runner leaves a plant from the Plant box alone for its first 2 minutes. During that time Save and Cancel it in Coming seeds work as they did before. The other option was editing a card's note after it is planted. That would need a new kind of request that rewrites the card's doc and title on main, which F90, Show the planted note, already turned down. The cost: when the factory is idle, an idea becomes a card, and "Plant and ask the lead" gets its answer, 2 minutes later. That is small next to how long a reply takes.
>    Action: f232-01, a lead ticket, because the runner's code is protected.
> 3. **Is 2 minutes the right length?**: I recommend 2 minutes: long enough to fix a typo or a note sent half-written, short enough not to slow down a question to the lead. The alternatives: 5 minutes, or no timer at all and a 'Send now' button on each waiting plant, which means more to build and one more thing for you to press.
>    Action: Answer only if you want 5 minutes or the button; otherwise 2 minutes stands.
> 4. **Messages on a thread are not held**: Messages you send on a thread can be edited the same way, and have the same short window when the factory is idle. I left them out: a hold would add 2 minutes to every reply in a planning conversation, and you asked about seeds. Seeds that come from a review's ideas are not held either: their words are the lead's, so they can only be cancelled.
>    Action: Nothing unless you want messages held too.
> 5. **The Coming row says when the plant goes, and there's no mock-up**: While the hold lasts, the row says 'planting in about 2 min' instead of 'planting at the runner's next pick'. That is a change of words only, so there's no mock-up.
>    Action: f232-02, a developer ticket, after f232-01.
