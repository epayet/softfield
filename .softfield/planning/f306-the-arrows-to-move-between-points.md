# F306: On a phone, the arrows to step through points stay put and headlines come first

The planning thread behind F306's plan, oldest first, as it was when the owner approved it.

## The owner, 2026-11-26 00:19

> The arrows to move between points in planning on mobile keep moving depending on the size of the point that's annoying to navigate when glancing through points because I have to find them again each time. The headlines are the most important parts

## The lead, 2026-11-26 01:02

> I've drafted a plan and a mock-up: on a phone, the arrows move above the points so they stay in one place, and each point's headline is larger and sits right under them. On a computer nothing changes. It's one small ticket for a developer, and the ticket check passes.
>
> 1. **Why the arrows move**: On a phone only one point shows at a time, and ◀ ▶ are drawn under it. So they move with the length of each point. On F305, Find what writes terabytes to the SSD, point 3 is long enough to push them under the Send box, and point 6 brings them back up.
>    Action: Ticket f306-01 moves them above the points, under the In short box. A reply's In short doesn't change while you step through its points, so the arrows stay in exactly the same place.
> 2. **Headline first, right under the arrows**: You said the headlines matter most. Today a headline is the same size as the text around it. In the plan it is larger, in the dashboard's own heading font, and it is the first thing under the arrows. Glancing through becomes tap, read the headline, tap, without your eyes or thumb moving.
>    Action: Same ticket. The mock-up shows it on F305's six real points.
> 3. **The catch with arrows on top, and how the plan handles it**: If you scroll down to read a long point, the arrows go off the top of the screen. When that happens, pressing ◀ or ▶ scrolls them back to just under the top bar, so the next headline appears where you are already looking. I'd rather do this than pin the arrows to the screen: the top bar is already pinned at the top and Send at the bottom, and a third pinned strip would leave little room for the point on a phone.
>    Action: Same ticket. If you'd rather have the arrows pinned anyway, say so.
> 4. **"Point 2 of 6" beside the dots**: With the arrows above the point, a short line of words saying where you are also helps. Today the dots are the only sign of your place.
>    Action: Same ticket. Comment if you'd rather keep only the dots.
> 5. **Reviews of plans get the same**: A plan's review page also shows its key points one at a time on a phone, with the same arrows under them. It changes the same way.
>    Action: Same ticket.
> 6. **Left out: a list of every headline**: To glance through even faster, the page could also show a list of all the headlines, or let you tap a dot to jump to a point. Fixed arrows plus a bigger headline should be enough, so I left both out to keep this small.
>    Action: Nothing now. Plant a card if it still feels slow after this one.
