> I've drafted a plan for this card: one developer ticket that makes every text box on the dashboard grow as you type. The ticket passes the pre-flight check, and nothing is queued until you approve it.
>
> 1. **Why the Plant box stays small**: The Plant box on the Planning page is fixed at four lines. A longer idea scrolls inside that small box, and nothing on the dashboard makes a box fit its text. The other text boxes are fixed the same way: the reply box on a card's thread, the box for changing a message you sent, the comment box under each key point (2 lines), and the edit box of a plant still waiting to be picked up.
>    Action: Ticket f82-01-text-boxes-grow.
> 2. **Every text box, not only Plant**: You asked about the Plant box, but the thread's reply and the key-point comments are smaller still (two lines), and you write long notes in them too. One rule for the whole dashboard fixes them all, and any box added later grows as well. It costs no more than fixing the Plant box alone.
>    Action: Part of f82-01.
> 3. **How far it grows**: A box starts at its current height and grows one line at a time as you type, up to about 60% of the screen. After that it scrolls inside, so on a phone the Plant and Plant and ask buttons stay close by. When you empty a box, or after you plant, it goes back to its starting height. If you'd rather it grow with no limit, say so.
>    Action: Part of f82-01.
> 4. **No mock-up for this card**: Nothing is added, moved or restyled: the same box just gets taller as you type, and a still picture can't show that better than the page itself will. Ask if you'd like a page you can type into to try the 60% limit first.
