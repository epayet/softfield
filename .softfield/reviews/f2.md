## Workflow observations
- **The last developer merge of a milestone goes unchecked.** Checkpoints come every 2 merges, so f2-07 and f2-13 merged with no checkpoint after them, and only the review read f2-13. With cheap checkpoints (≈$0.25), checking the leftovers before the review looks worth an ADR (suggested under "What next").
- **Cloud developers fail fast.** gpt-oss-120b's five lost attempts on f2-06 took minutes, not GPU half-hours. That makes failure cheap, but they still need tickets inside their known range. Bench them before giving them the role, as planned.
- **The 25% rule parked work exactly as designed** (78% → parked). The cost was that nothing else could run. Planning has to give the queue developer work, or lead-first only means waiting first.
- **No `f2-start` tag existed** for the review. Only a manual step created it, and F2's plan session skipped it. Now `runner milestone` creates it (f2-F7).
