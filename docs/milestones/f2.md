> **Status: planned** (2026-10-02), waiting for [ADR 16](../adr/16-claude-window-and-named-milestones.md) to be accepted. From the [F1 review](../reviews/factory-f1.md) and the owner's notes after F1. Next: [F3](f3.md), the factory line.

## Outcome
- Claude's 5-hour window is shared well: unattended work stops at 75% and keeps the rest for the owner; lead work comes first, and Claude takes developer tickets only when no lead work waits (ADR 16).
- Ollama Cloud developers are benched and put to work: a developer ticket goes to the best permitted cloud agent, then local.
- The dashboard always says what the runner is doing and which ticket waits for what; Ctrl-C leaves nothing behind; the Overview shows every agent's allowance; every job records its exact model.
- Local attempts stuck in a thinking loop are cut early.
- Milestones have descriptive keys (`f3-factory-line`).

## Decisions
- **D1 Config:** `[claude]` in `workflow/runner.toml` gains `five_hour_reserve = 0.25` (the owner's share, ADR 16 item 1) and `developer_five_hour_cap = 0.5` (item 2); `Config` reads both.
- **D2 Where the rules live:** the 5-hour window's reserve is `allowance.Window.reserve` on the `five_hour` window (so `allowance.answer` stays the one rule; a rolling window with a reserve caps at `capacity − reserve` unless `use_reserve`). Lead-first is the router's (`runner/router.py`): `route` gains whether lead work is ready.
- **D3 `jobs.model`:** a new migration; the adapters return the model each job ran on (Claude: the stream's `model`; OpenCode: the agent's `model`). Backfill fills it from the logs where it can.
- **D4 Named keys:** ADR 16 item 6. F2 itself keeps the key `f2`; F3 becomes `f3-factory-line` after f2-01 (renamed by the lead in the next session).
