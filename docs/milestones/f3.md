## Outcome
- `/milestones/<key>/line` shows a milestone as a factory floor at one moment (`?at=`), drawn from one JSON document that holds every rule.
- **MVP only** (owner, 2026-10-02): the floor at one moment. Replay, the other tabs, the phone layout and the Overview-as-factory come later (below).

## Decisions

- **D5 Charts:** a `<div data-chart="<kind>">` followed by a `<script type="application/json">` data block; `factory_dashboard/static/charts.js` has one function per kind, drawing with ECharts. Tests check the JSON a route renders, not the drawing; the review takes screenshots.
- **D10 The factory line** (the owner, 2026-10-02; the picture is [`f1-factory-mockup.html`](f1-factory-mockup.html), v18, not a file to read):
  - **Where:** the Overview (`/`) becomes the factory, live: Needs-you (only when needed), the floor of the current milestone, the Now panel, then allowances, savings and spend. Running now and Queue as separate panels go. Each milestone has `/milestones/<key>/line`, which replays it in four tabs: Factory floor, Dependencies, Production timeline (replacing `/timeline`) and Plan vs actual. The top bar gets "Factory".
  - **Data, all in Python:** `factory_dashboard/line.py` owns `factory_line(conn, project, milestone)`, a JSON document (D5 style) that the drawing only renders: no rule lives in JavaScript. Tested in `tests/factory_dashboard/test_line.py`. `queries.run_order(conn, project)` (in `factory_dashboard/queries.py`) is `pick_next`'s rule (`runner/tasks.py`) applied repeatedly to the DB's `ready` rows: the order the runner will take them, with "ready now" or "after f1-xx".
  - **Machines:** one per agent in the project's `workflow/runner.toml` that a role names or a job used (`runner.config.load_config` on `projects.repo`), with its name, LOCAL (harness on the GPU) or CLOUD, its `rating` (the latest `rating_changes.after` once f1-24 writes them), and its side: **the lead is one machine**, "The lead (Claude)", with one plate per Claude model it runs on (Opus, Sonnet) and the active one lit; developers share one developer bay; a shadow agent (Copilot, f1-26) has its own blue machine fed by the same checkpoint and "not rated yet" without a rating. Stronger agents are bigger and have a bigger chimney.
  - **Stations and belts** (ids fixed): stations `queue`, the machines, `checks` (the runner's gates, not an agent), `merged`. Belts: `queue→dev` "developer tickets", `queue→lead` "lead tickets", `dev→checks` "attempt done: to checks", `lead→checks` "lead's work: to checks", `checks→merged` "passed: merged", `checks→dev` "failed: retry", `checks→lead` "failed twice: direction · 3 times: escalation", `lead→dev` "a direction: retry", `lead→queue` "back to the queue (findings, requeued, parked)", `merged→lead` "checkpoints, milestone review", `merged→shadow` "same checkpoint".
  - **Trips, from the jobs:** a ticket's first job takes it from the queue to its machine; a job that passes and is the ticket's last goes `→checks→merged`; a failed developer attempt goes `→checks` then `checks→dev` (a retry) or `checks→lead` (a direction or an escalation is next); a direction goes `lead→dev`; a lead ticket parked, interrupted or requeued, and an escalation that requeues, go `lead→queue` and come back on `queue→lead`/`queue→dev`; a checkpoint or review job (no ticket) is a batch crate `merged→lead→queue`. Directions and findings are text, so they skip checks. The JSON lists every trip as `{at, crate, belts, to}`, time-ordered, plus each belt's last and next trip.
  - **What the page shows:** crates coloured by kind (lead orange, developer green), faded and dashed while waiting on a dependency, numbered in run order in the Queue; Merged in completion order; the working ticket's path lit (the belt it came on solid, its way to Merged dashed); a belt rolls only while a crate travels on it; limit windows grey the lead with "Claude limit reached · resets HH:MM"; the Checks gate flashes green or red. Play steps one event at a time (5, 3 or 1.5 s), ◀ ▶ step by hand, no zoom. Hover or tap a crate: its details and its dependencies outlined; a belt: its last and next trip. Under 640 px a portrait drawing fits the width (developers stacked), with "the same, in words" below and a sticky Play bar.
  - **Idle stretches** over 40 min are drawn shorter on the timeline and plan tabs, with their real length written on them.
  - **The drawing:** `factory_dashboard/static/factory.js`, hand-written SVG, no new vendor file; smoke and animation honour `prefers-reduced-motion` (still puffs). The review checks it with screenshots on desktop and phone (D5).
