# Milestone 2: Device pairing and authentication

> **Status: in progress.** Base: git tag `m2-start`. Only the owner marks this milestone done.
>
> Pairing details are in [ADR 03](../adr/03-m2-pairing-details.md): Accepted by the owner on 2026-09-28.

## Outcome

A browser opens `/`, makes its key, and shows a pairing code and fingerprint in English or French. The owner claims the device in admin and decides there whether it's a display (assigned to a space) or a phone (assigned to a person). `/` then leads to `/display/` or `/phone/`. From then on it gets a session silently and re-authenticates by itself on 401. Revoking it cuts it off at once.

| `m2-03-all-set-page` | The page after pairing ("Waiting for the first moment" / "You're in") | outcome |
| `m2-04-language-choice` | `pick_language(...)`: the ADR 00 A2 rules as a plain function | outcome |

**Experiment:** m2-03 and m2-04 say *what* to build, not *how*. The session narrative compares their first-attempt pass, attempts, model time and diff size with m2-01 and m2-02. It's a signal, not a verdict: four tasks is a tiny sample.

## Done when

- All tasks are in `tasks/done/`, every gate passes on `main`, and `pytest -m e2e` passes on staging.
- The milestone review ends, and `docs/reviews/m2.md` is written, including the French check and `runner commits --milestone 2`.
- The owner captures the signature vectors (Chrome + iPhone Safari), pairs a real Android display and a real iPhone over Tailscale, and signs off.
