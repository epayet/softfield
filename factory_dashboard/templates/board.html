The board

Every feature starts as a seed in the field. Planning sprouts it, the factory grows it ticket by ticket, you harvest it at sign-off. Each card shows its place in its stage: #1 is first in line. Drag a card by its grip, or use ⋯, to change the order inside a stage; nothing is sent until you press Send new order, and the runner follows it from its next pick.
